# Code of Conduct

MCPShield follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and focus on what is best for the
community. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@nullvora.com**. Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainers,
who may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour violates this code.
